<h1 align="center">📊 Swiggy Instamart Sales Performance Dashboard</h1>

<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>
<p>
This project presents an interactive <b>Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard</b> built using Power BI.
The dashboard analyzes sales data from Swiggy Instamart to uncover insights related to sales performance,
customer orders, product demand, and customer ratings.
</p>

<p>
The objective of this project is to transform raw data into meaningful insights using
data visualization and business intelligence techniques.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🎯 Project Objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>Analyze overall sales performance.</li>
<li>Understand order distribution across different city tiers.</li>
<li>Identify top-performing product categories.</li>
<li>Evaluate customer satisfaction through ratings.</li>
<li>Compare performance of different shop types.</li>
</ul>

<h2>📈 Dashboard Features</h2>

<h3>1. Sales by City Type</h3>
<p>
Bar chart showing total sales distribution across <b>Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities</b>.
</p>

<h3>2. Orders by City Tier</h3>
<p>
Donut chart visualizing how total orders are distributed across city tiers.
</p>

<h3>3. Sales by Product Category</h3>
<p>
Horizontal bar chart highlighting top selling product categories such as:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Fruits & Vegetables</li>
<li>Snack Foods</li>
<li>Household Items</li>
<li>Frozen Foods</li>
<li>Dairy Products</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. Shop Opening Year Analysis</h3>
<p>
Line chart showing shop expansion trends over the years.
</p>

<h3>5. Shop Size Distribution</h3>
<p>
Donut chart displaying shop size categories such as <b>Small, Medium, and High</b>.
</p>

<h3>6. Shop Type Performance</h3>
<p>
Table comparing shop types based on:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Total Sales</li>
<li>Total Orders</li>
<li>Average Rating</li>
<li>Average Sales</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>🔎 Filters Available</h2>
